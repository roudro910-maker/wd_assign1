A Day in the Life at Hawthorn

To give you an authentic look at what it feels like to study here, I put together a quick one-minute video guide outlining your morning arrival, study routines, and the best place to refuel between classes.

Video Breakdown & Highlights

  • 0:00 - 0:20 (The Arrival): Arriving at Glenferrie Station. It is incredibly easy to get here because the platforms lead straight into the campus footprint.
  • 0:20 - 0:45 (The Study Hub): Setting up inside the 24/7 Late Lab building. Getting to Level 2 early gives you a perfect desk to finish off work before lectures.
  • 0:45 - 1:05 (The Social Spot): Hanging out at 'The Atrium Cafe' right inside the campus walls for budget-friendly coffee, meals, and socializing.
